The Engine

Record it. Scatter it. Get dirt.

PARTICULA is a granular-cloud signal engine. It records whatever you feed it, then plays a wandering playhead back through that recording as a cloud of particles. Every grain is time-stretched — a fixed value — while its pitch and frequency are bent by an LFO. And each particle is written back into the history, so the cloud feeds on itself and grows into an abstract, gritty texture.

Particles are fired at a set interval, drawn from a pool, and given a random lifetime. No two runs are ever the same — and that's the point. This is a tool built for random sounds and experimental sound design.

Features

Built for weird, gritty texture

Everything you need to turn a recording into a living cloud of bent, stretched, random particles.

01

Record & Playhead

PARTICULA records the input, then plays a wandering playhead back through it. Each grain is a slice of that recording, replayed at its own time and position.

RecordPlayheadGrains
02

I · Spawn

Interval fires a new particle at a set rate; Pool caps how many particles live at once; Fallback writes each grain back into the recorded history. Every particle gets a random lifetime.

IntervalPoolFallback
03

I · Movement

Particle position isn't fixed — it wanders. Fixed holds still, LFO lets an oscillator drive it, Walk takes a random stroll, and Peak hunts for the nearest loud spot.

FixedLFOWalkPeak
04

Stretch · Pitch · Shift

Every grain time-stretches the source by a fixed value, while an LFO drives its pitch and frequency shifts — bending the recording into something abstract.

StretchPitchShift
